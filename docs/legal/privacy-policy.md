## 4. The AI coach

If you switch on AI data sharing, we send the information the coach needs (your setup answers, hustle,
roadmap progress, check-ins with attached screenshots, and your messages) to **Anthropic PBC** (USA), which
provides the Claude AI models, to generate replies, check-in analysis and a personalized roadmap. Anthropic
processes this data on our behalf and does not use data sent through its API to train its models. You can
switch AI data sharing off at any time in Profile → Privacy & legal; after that nothing more is sent.
AI replies can be wrong and are not financial, tax, investment or legal advice.

## 5. Analytics and session recordings

Only if you agree, we use **PostHog** (servers in the EU) to understand how the app is used: screen views,
important actions (e.g. finishing a step, starting a trial) and, in the iPhone app, recordings of app sessions
in which **every text field and every image is hidden**. We identify you by your account ID only (not your
name or email), we do not collect IP-based location or money amounts, and we do not use this data for
advertising. You can switch analytics off at any time in Profile → Privacy & legal.

## 7. How long we keep data

We keep your data as long as you have an account. When you delete your account (Profile → Delete account &
data), we delete your account, answers, check-ins, screenshots, coach messages and progress from our
servers immediately, and ask RevenueCat to delete your purchase history. Apple keeps its own records of your
purchases. Analytics data is kept by PostHog for up to [12] months. Backups are overwritten within [30] days.
